**Fanimate 0.2.1** makes damaged projects and some unusual brush strokes safe to open. It is still an alpha: keep backups, and please [report what goes wrong](https://github.com/toonittmedia/fanimate/issues).

## Fixes

- **Damaged projects open instead of crashing.** A new test damages projects in thousands of ways and opens each one. It found three ways a damaged `.fnm` could crash Fanimate, now fixed:
  - a shape whose points are not numbers, or lie impossibly far away, is now skipped (and listed by the validator) instead of crashing the renderer;
  - layer ids at the very top of their range are renumbered when the project is repaired, so adding a layer can no longer overflow;
  - see the next point.
- **Brush strokes that turn sharply back on themselves always draw.** The stroke renderer could produce broken geometry for a variable-width stroke that doubles back on itself, such as a tight scribble. Such a stroke is now drawn at an even width instead of being lost.

## Behind the scenes

- **Crash recovery is tested 100 times per run:** a process writing a project is killed outright at random moments, and the recovered project must open cleanly with the last finished change every time.
- **Licence audit:** every one of the 501 Rust crates Fanimate ships is checked for an MIT/Apache-compatible licence on each release; the record is in [docs/legal/licences.md](https://github.com/toonittmedia/fanimate/blob/main/docs/legal/licences.md).
- `fanimate-cli bench --seconds 300` times opening a 5-minute scene, and `fanimate-cli sample episode` writes one.
- First Gate 1 measurements are in the [Phase 1 roadmap](https://github.com/toonittmedia/fanimate/blob/main/docs/roadmap/phase-1.md#53-results-so-far): a 5-minute scene loads in about 0.1 s, renders match across Windows, macOS and Linux to within 1/255, and updating an installed copy works on Windows.
